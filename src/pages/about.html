About TX2

A Union company that picks up the phone.

TX2 Services started with one truck, one trailer and a simple idea: people around Franklin County shouldn't have to call a St. Louis call center to get a dumpster in their driveway. We're local, we're small enough to care, and we're big enough to show up.

How we work

Three things we won't compromise on.

1. You talk to the person doing the work.

When you call {{phone}}, you get someone who knows the schedule, knows the trucks and can give you a real answer. No ticket numbers, no "someone will call you back."

2. The price we quote is the price you pay.

Delivery, pickup, five days and a ton — one number. If a job's going to run heavy (shingles, concrete) we tell you before the dumpster leaves the yard, not on the invoice.

3. We treat your property like it's ours.

Boards under every dumpster. Trailers for soft ground. Doors set the direction you asked for. Gate closed when we leave. It costs us a few extra minutes and it's why people call back.
