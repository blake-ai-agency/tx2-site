Pricing, plainly

One flat number. No mystery line items.

Your quote covers delivery, placement on boards, pickup, up to five days on site and one ton of disposal at the landfill. That's it. If you go over on days or weight, it's a simple flat add-on that we'll tell you about before you book — not after.

  • Extra days: flat daily rate. Call before day five and we'll extend it.
  • Extra weight: billed per ton at the landfill rate, rounded to the nearest 100 lb.
  • Heavy materials (shingles, concrete, dirt, brick): priced by the load so you're not surprised at the scale.
  • Second haul: filled it up early? We'll swap it out and keep your project moving.

Pricing varies a little by town — Union and Washington are our shortest runs — so call or text with your address for an exact number.

Before we drop it

Prep checklist.

Where should the dumpster go?

Flat, firm ground with about 60 feet of straight approach and 14 feet of overhead clearance (watch for low branches and power lines). Driveways are ideal. If it has to go on the street, check with your city — Union, Washington and Pacific may require a permit for street placement; we'll help you sort it out.

How long can I keep it?

Five days are included. Need longer? Call before day five and we'll extend at a flat daily rate. Need it gone early? We'll come get it.

How much weight is included?

One ton (2,000 lb). Household junk, wood and drywall rarely go over. Shingles, concrete, dirt, tile and brick get heavy fast — tell us up front and we'll price the load right.

How full can I load it?

Level with the top rail. We tarp every load for the drive to the landfill, so anything sticking above the rail has to come off before we can haul it.

What can't go in?

Tires, paint and solvents, oil and fuel, batteries, propane tanks, asbestos, medical or hazardous waste, and appliances with refrigerant still in them. Mattresses, TVs and electronics may carry a small landfill surcharge. Full list here.
