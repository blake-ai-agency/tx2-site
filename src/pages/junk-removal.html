Junk removal questions

How do you price junk removal?

By volume — how much of the truck or dumpster your stuff fills — plus any landfill surcharges for items like mattresses, TVs or refrigerant appliances. Photos get you a firm number up front; we confirm it on site before we start.

Do I need to be there?

For outdoor jobs, no — just tell us what's going. For inside cleanouts we like someone there at the start to walk us through what stays.

Can you handle a whole house?

Yes. Most whole-home cleanouts in Franklin County take us one to two days. For big estates we'll often drop a 14-yard dumpster and load it with a crew — the cheapest way to move a lot of volume.

What don't you take?

Hazardous materials — paint, chemicals, oil, asbestos, medical waste — and anything a landfill won't accept. Full list. If you're not sure, ask; we know where most of it can go.
