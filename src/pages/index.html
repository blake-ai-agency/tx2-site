Straight answers

Questions we get every day.

How much does a 14-yard dumpster cost?

One flat rate covers delivery, pickup, up to 5 days and 1 ton of disposal. The exact number depends on your town and what's going in it (shingles and concrete are priced differently than household junk). Call or text {{phone}} with your address and we'll quote it in minutes — no forms, no callbacks.

What can't I put in the dumpster?

No tires, paint, batteries, chemicals, oil, propane tanks, asbestos, medical waste or refrigerators with freon still in them. Mattresses, TVs and some electronics may carry a small landfill fee. See the full list.

Will the dumpster damage my driveway?

We set every roll-off on wooden boards so the steel rails never sit directly on your concrete or asphalt. For gravel, grass or soft ground we'll usually recommend the trailer dumpster, which sits on rubber tires.

How fast can you deliver?

Usually within a day or two, sometimes same-day if a unit frees up nearby. Weekends fill up first — if you've got a Saturday project, book by Wednesday.

Do I need to be there for delivery?

No. Tell us where you want it (a photo with an X works great) and which way the door should face. We'll text you when it's down.
