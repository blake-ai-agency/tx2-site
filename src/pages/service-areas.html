Good to know

Delivery notes by area.

Street placement & permits

Union, Washington, Pacific and most St. Louis County municipalities want a permit if a dumpster sits on a public street. Driveway placement avoids it entirely. We'll tell you what your town requires when you book.

Rural & gravel

Out toward Leslie, Beaufort, Lonedell and the river bottoms we'll usually bring the trailer dumpster — rubber tires, no ruts, and it gets into places a roll-off truck won't.

Outer-ring scheduling

Cuba, Potosi, Hermann and Troy are longer runs, so we batch them a few days a week. Book a couple of days ahead and you'll get the same flat-rate structure.
